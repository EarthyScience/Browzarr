The colorbar below the plot maps data values to colors and doubles as a control surface.

## Lower and upper bounds

You can control the lower and upper bounds of your displayed data by clicking and typing different numbers in the input boxes (extreme left and right).

### Shift values

Dragging the colorbar itself will also shift the bound values, preserving the range width.

## Colormaps

Hover the colormap options in the main menu to preview how your plot will change, then click the one you like to select it.

## Transfer Functions

Clicking on the cog to the left of the colorbar will open the transfer function menu. Here you can enter equations to scale and
modulate the data to further highlight values and features of interest. **not availabe in bivariate mode** 

## Univariate vs bivariate

By default the colorbar maps one variable. In **bivariate mode** two variables are mapped to a 2D color scheme and the colorbar becomes a two-dimensional strip — see [Bivariate colormaps](/guides/bivariate-colormaps).
